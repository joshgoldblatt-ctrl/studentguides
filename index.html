How to Access · Step 1

Open Navigate & Tap the App Grid

In Navigate, tap the grid icon at the bottom of your screen to open the app menu.

Look for the 🔀 Journeys icon — it's the purple tile. Tap it!

📲 Tap the grid → Tap Journeys
How to Access · Step 2

See Your List of Journeys

You'll see your current Journeys listed with progress bars.

You may have a Transfer & Career Journey and a major-specific one — check both!

How to Access · Steps 3 & 4

Open a Journey → Mark Steps Complete

Tap a journey to see its checklist of steps with details and due dates.

Tap a step → select "This is completed" → press Save. Done! ✅

🚀

Your Future Starts Now.

Open Navigate today and start your Journey!

  • Check off any steps you've completed — don't worry if there's nothing to check off yet
  • Advisors, career services, and other faculty and staff are here to help you
  • You don't need to follow the order — check off steps as you complete them
